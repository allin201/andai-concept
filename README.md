# 安垈 · Andai

> 一个关于末地更新的纯概念构想 · A concept for an End update

## 简介 / Introduction

安垈是一次关于末地更新的纯概念构想：如果末地也有自己的「草原」、自己的木材、自己的逆向流体，以及自己的伤害循环效果，会是什么样子？下面所有内容都尽量保持末地原有的气质——苍白、干燥、带一点不祥。

*Andai is a pure concept for a Minecraft End update: what if the End had its own “grassland,” its own wood, its own inverted fluid, and its own damage-looping effects — while still feeling unmistakably like the End? Everything below keeps that palette: pale, dry, and faintly ominous.*

## 内容 / Contents

### 安垈坪 · Andai Turf

一种特殊的末地石，顶面有一层类似主世界草方块草皮的纹理，颜色为焦黄色。挖掉后变回普通末地石；用附有精准采集的物品可以直接采集本体。

*A special End Stone block whose top face carries a grass-like overlay, like the turf on an Overworld grass block, but charred yellow. Mining it reverts it to ordinary End Stone; a Silk Touch item collects the block itself.*

### 安垈原木 · Andai Log

一种淡灰色的原木类方块，纹理与普通橡木原木十分相似，可分解为四块安垈木板。

*A pale grey log block, textured much like an ordinary oak log. It crafts into four Andai Planks.*

### 安垈木板 · Andai Planks

用途与普通木板无异，材质为淡灰色。

*Identical in use to ordinary planks, with a pale grey texture.*

### 安垈木种的常规配方 · Standard Wood Recipes

安垈原木可替代任意原木参与全部常规木类配方；成品除外观外，功能与其他木种几乎无异。

*Andai Log substitutes for any log in all standard wood recipes; the results are functionally identical to those of other wood types, differing only in appearance.*

### 安垈草 · Andai Grass

看起来像焦黄色的主世界草丛，但无法直接挖掉；它仍会接收每一次挖掘的第一下尝试，相当于一个无敌、可穿过的隐身实体。

破坏方式包括：破坏其下方的方块、用水流冲走、爆炸、火焰、创造模式指令等。破坏后不掉落任何物品，也无法用骨粉扩散。

*Looks like charred-yellow Overworld grass, but cannot be mined directly. It still registers the first attempt of every mining action — effectively an invincible, walk-through invisible entity.*

*It can be removed by breaking the block beneath it, washing it away with water, explosions, fire, creative-mode commands, and the like. It drops nothing and cannot be spread with Bone Meal.*

### 安垈灌 · Andai Canopy

一种叶类方块，纹理与普通橡木叶几乎无差别，颜色为焦黄色。与其他叶类方块不同，安垈灌同时具有固体与液体性质：生物可以穿过它，也可以停留在它内部。

穿过或停留在其中时，生物移速降低 15%，并被反复赋予 1 秒的「垈惰」效果。末影珍珠投入其中会直接消失，不产生传送。

*A leaf-type block, nearly indistinguishable from ordinary oak leaves in texture, but charred yellow. Unlike other leaves, Andai Canopy behaves as both a solid and a fluid: entities can pass through it and can also be contained inside it.*

*While inside or passing through, an entity’s movement speed is reduced by 15% and it is repeatedly given a 1-second “Daiduo” effect. Ender Pearls thrown into it vanish without teleporting the thrower.*

### 安果 · Andai Fruit

贴图是一枚旧版贴图的淡灰色苹果。一枚安果可食用两次：第一次食用不产生任何效果，但物品栏中的图标会变成被咬过一半的样子；第二次食用后，食用者被传送回第一次食用时的位置，安果随即消失，食用者损失三颗心（6 点生命值），并恢复 2.5 点饥饿值。

若第一次食用时食用者位于安垈灌内，会立即原地爆炸。

若食用者在两次食用之间死亡，被咬过一半的安果直接消失。

不可叠加。

*Textured as a pale grey apple in the old apple style. One Andai Fruit can be eaten twice. The first bite has no effect, but its inventory icon changes to a half-eaten apple. On the second bite the eater is teleported back to the position of the first bite, the fruit disappears, the eater loses three hearts (6 health points), and 2.5 hunger points are restored.*

*If the eater is inside Andai Canopy when taking the first bite, they explode on the spot immediately.*

*If the eater dies between the two bites, the half-eaten fruit disappears.*

*Cannot be stacked.*

### 安垈苗 · Andai Sapling

可种植在安垈坪上，无法用骨粉催熟；经过 2~3 个游戏日后长成安垈丛。

*Can be planted on Andai Turf. Bone Meal does not speed it up. After 2–3 in-game days it grows into an Andai Grove.*

### 安垈丛 · Andai Grove

由安垈原木与安垈灌组成的自然植株结构，通常包含1到3根安垈原木（排列往往并不对齐）与12到16块安垈灌。

*A natural plant structure made of Andai Logs and Andai Canopy: usually 1–3 Andai Logs (often not aligned) together with 12–16 Andai Canopy blocks.*

### 安垈 · Andai (Biome)

生成概率约为每 130 个末地区块中有至少一处：以一个存在「接触空气的末地石」的区块作为群系中心，再随机扩散 3~8 次。

该范围内的末地石会有一部分被替换为安垈坪；安垈坪与普通末地石的交界呈雪花噪点状过渡。在连片的安垈坪上，会随机生成安垈草、安垈丛、紫颂植株等植株结构。

*Generation chance is roughly at least one occurrence per 130 End chunks: pick a chunk containing air-exposed End Stone as the biome centre, then spread randomly 3–8 times.*

*Some End Stone in the area is replaced by Andai Turf, and the boundary between Andai Turf and ordinary End Stone transitions like snow noise. On connected patches of Andai Turf, Andai Grass, Andai Groves, Chorus Plant and similar plant structures generate at random.*

### 安垈药水 · Potion of Daiduo

以安果为酿造材料酿出的灰色药水。与普通药水一样有三种形态——可饮用、喷溅、滞留。使用后目标获得「垈惰」效果：一级 30 秒，二级 1 分钟，三级 1 分 30 秒。

*A grey potion brewed with Andai Fruit. Like ordinary potions it comes in three forms — drinkable, splash and lingering. The target gains the “Daiduo” effect: Level I for 30 seconds, Level II for 1 minute, Level III for 1 minute 30 seconds.*

### 垈惰 · Daiduo

拥有该效果者无法被任何形式的传送效果干扰或作用。

*While affected, the entity cannot be moved or otherwise affected by any form of teleportation.*

### 挧地石 · Yudi Stone

一种末地石，颜色比普通末地石更偏肉色。普通挖掘不会掉落物品，而是在被挖掉的位置生成一种叫「挧沫」的液体；用附有精准采集的物品可以直接采集本体。

*An End Stone-like block, flesh-toned rather than the usual pale yellow. Mining it normally drops nothing; instead it spawns a liquid called “Yumo” in the mined position. A Silk Touch item collects the block itself.*

### 挧沫 · Yumo

一种淡肉色的、非无限液体。挧沫的重力方向与其他所有液体相反：它的流动部分向上延伸，处于流动部分中的个体会被带着向上走。末影人不受这种液体影响。

出于崩溃风险考虑，挧沫的流动部分最多延伸 16 格；每多延伸一格，贴图就更透明一些。若放置在世界的最高搭建高度时，它会保持静止态；如果是它的流动部分，那么将直接在到达限制高度后被删除。

挧沫接触水时会被水同化。

接触流动的岩浆时生成末地石；接触静止的岩浆时使岩浆变成黑曜石。

可用铁桶盛装，但盛装后它在物品栏中的图标会上下颠倒。

*A pale flesh-coloured, non-infinite fluid. Yumo’s gravity is inverted relative to every other liquid: its flowing portion moves upward, and entities caught in it are carried upward. Endermen are unaffected by it.*

*To avoid crash risks, flowing Yumo extends at most 16 blocks; each extra block is rendered more transparent. If placed at the world's build height limit, it remains static. A flowing portion, however, is deleted upon reaching that height limit.*

*On contact with water, Yumo is assimilated by the water.*

*On contact with flowing lava it creates End Stone; on contact with still lava it turns the lava into Obsidian.*

*It can be collected in an iron bucket, but the collected item’s inventory icon is flipped upside down.*

### 挧洼 · Yumo Pool

类似于主世界小水洼的自然结构，但其中生成的液体是挧沫；周围有概率生成挧叶甘。

*A natural structure resembling a small Overworld puddle, except the liquid inside is Yumo. Yuye Cane may generate around it.*

### 挧叶甘 · Yuye Cane

一种深肉色的植株，贴图类似旧版甘蔗。可种植在邻近水源的末地石上。它的每一节都会使周围 13×13（水平面）范围内产生淡白色的 3 像素竖线粒子；范围内所有完整水源方块保持静止，流动液体消失。

拆掉后效果范围消失，本应流动的液体恢复流动。无法徒手采集，只能用附有精准采集的工具挖掘获得。

*A dark flesh-coloured plant textured like old-style sugar cane. It can be planted on End Stone near a water source. Each segment makes pale white 3-pixel vertical-line particles appear across the surrounding 13×13 horizontal area. Within that area all full water source blocks stay still and flowing liquids vanish.*

*When removed, the area effect ends and liquids that should flow resume flowing. It cannot be obtained by hand; only a Silk Touch tool collects it.*

### 挧湿地 · Yumo Wetland

生成概率约为每 130 个末地区块中有至少一处：以一个存在「接触空气的末地石」的区块作为群系中心，再随机扩散 3~5 次。

该群系中的末地石上会不连续地形成一片片「挧沫」，并更高概率生成「挧洼」自然结构……以及紫颂植株。

*Generation chance is roughly at least one occurrence per 130 End chunks: pick a chunk containing air-exposed End Stone as the biome centre, then spread randomly 3–5 times.*

*In this biome, disconnected patches of Yumo form on the End Stone, and Yumo Pools generate more often… along with Chorus Plant.*

### 岾苔块 · Jiedi Moss Block

一种贴图类似淡紫色苔藓块的方块，挖掘后掉落自身。

*A block textured like a pale purple moss block. Mining it drops itself.*

### 岾苔藓 · Jiedi Moss

一种非完整方块，贴图像淡紫色苔藓。挖掘后 80% 概率什么都不掉，20% 概率掉落「岾苔团」；用附有精准采集的工具可直接采集本体。

*A non-full block textured like pale purple moss. Mining it drops nothing 80% of the time and a “Jiedi Moss Ball” 20% of the time; a Silk Touch tool collects the block itself.*

### 岾苔团 · Jiedi Moss Ball

一种食物，贴图是一团紫色苔藓。食用后，食用者被传送到当前 y 轴向下第一个可站立的方块上，恢复 2 点饥饿值，损失 1.5 点生命值（0.75 颗心）。

下方为虚空时无法食用；若下方第一个方块是安垈灌，食用会立即原地爆炸。

*A food item textured as a clump of purple moss. On eating, the eater is teleported down to the first standable block below their current Y level, regains 2 hunger points, and loses 1.5 health points (0.75 hearts).*

*It cannot be eaten when the space below is void. If the first block below is Andai Canopy, eating it causes an immediate explosion on the spot.*

### 岾地花 · Jiedi Flower

外形像主世界苔藓洞窟里的垂滴叶，但为淡紫色，通常呈未开放的花苞状，并直接生长在地面上。有实体踩到它、随后离开后，它会开放。

开放后，其周围 15×15×15 范围内持续产生淡紫色孢子粒子；范围内的所有腾空行为都被禁止，例外只有三种：从高处坠落、走楼梯与台阶、骑乘生物。也就是说：无法跳跃；滑翔中的玩家被强制取消滑翔；飞行生物失去飞行能力；漂浮效果失效；投掷物垂直落地，等等。

开放状态通常持续 5 分钟后变回花苞，同时特殊范围消失；也可以用红石持续保持开放。普通挖掘不掉落，但用附有精准采集的工具可以采集。

*Looks like the dripleaf of Overworld moss caves, but pale purple, usually in an unopened bud state, and growing directly on the ground. When an entity steps on it and then moves away, it opens.*

*While open, it continuously emits pale purple spore particles throughout a 15×15×15 area around itself. Inside that area all airborne movement is forbidden, with only three exceptions: falling from a height, walking on stairs and slabs, and riding an entity. In other words: jumping is impossible; a gliding player is forced out of gliding; flying mobs lose flight; Levitation fails; projectiles drop straight down; and so on.*

*An open flower normally lasts five minutes before returning to a bud, at which point the area effect ends; Redstone can also hold it open. Normal mining drops nothing, but a Silk Touch tool collects it.*

### 岾毁的末地沉船 · Jiedi-Wrecked End Ship

生成概率约为每 150 个末地区块中有至少一处：以一个存在「接触空气的末地石」的区块作为结构中心。

外形像一艘放大的、被毁坏的末地船，毁坏程度类似主世界海洋中的沉船。结构内会生成大量岾类方块、战利品宝箱，以及 60% 概率生成鞘翅。

*Generation chance is roughly at least one occurrence per 150 End chunks: pick a chunk containing air-exposed End Stone as the structure centre.*

*It resembles an enlarged, wrecked End Ship, damaged much like an Overworld shipwreck. The structure contains many Jiedi-type blocks, loot chests, and a 60% chance of an Elytra.*

### 彁液块 · Geyue Fluid Block

一种深紫色、半透明的固液共存方块，贴图类似红石块。它提供的红石信号强度为 32 格，即最多可点亮 32 格红石线（原版上限为 15，需要模组扩展）。

生物穿过时会被不间断地赋予 30 秒「彁裂」效果。挖掘需要钻石镐，用其他工具挖掘不掉落。一个彁液块可分解为 9 个彁石粉。

*A dark purple, semi-transparent block that is simultaneously solid and fluid, textured like a Redstone Block. It provides a Redstone signal strength of 32 — enough to power 32 blocks of redstone dust (vanilla caps at 15, so a mod would have to raise the limit).*

*Entities passing through it are continuously given a 30-second “Geyue Fracture” effect. It requires a diamond pickaxe; mining it with anything else drops nothing. One Geyue Fluid Block breaks down into 9 Geyue Dust.*

### 彁石粉 · Geyue Dust

一种深紫色、类似红石粉的非完整方块。它能传导红石信号，可以竖直贴在墙面上，但不会与红石粉自动连接；可通过中继器与普通红石线路连接。生物接触后会不间断地获得 5 秒「彁裂」效果。

*A dark purple non-full block resembling Redstone Dust. It conducts Redstone signals and can be placed vertically on walls, but does not connect to Redstone Dust; it can link to ordinary Redstone circuitry through a repeater. Entities touching it are continuously given a 5-second “Geyue Fracture” effect.*

### 彁锥 · Geyue Spire

一种由彁液块构成的锥形结构，大小通常在11到23个彁液块之间；一个大型彁锥周围通常有2到3个更小的彁锥。

*A cone-shaped structure built from Geyue Fluid Blocks, usually 11–23 blocks in size. A large Geyue Spire usually has 2–3 smaller spires around it.*

### 彁谷 · Geyue Valley

生成概率约为每 100 个末地区块中有至少一处：以一个存在「接触空气的末地石」的区块作为结构中心。

地形类似主世界的峡谷，但峡谷间距约为主世界峡谷的2到3倍。谷内两侧每隔20到30格生成一个彁锥结构；谷内及谷上方两侧的表面方块上会随机生成一些彁石粉……以及紫颂植株。

*Generation chance is roughly at least one occurrence per 100 End chunks: pick a chunk containing air-exposed End Stone as the structure centre.*

*The terrain resembles an Overworld canyon, but canyon spacing is about 2–3 times that of Overworld canyons. Inside the valley, a Geyue Spire generates on each side every 20–30 blocks. On surface blocks inside the valley and on both upper slopes, Geyue Dust generates at random… along with Chorus Plant.*

### 彁裂 · Geyue Fracture

拥有该效果者，在效果持续期间每次受到伤害，都会被随机传送到周围 7×7×7 范围内的任意位置；该传送可以被安垈灌拦截。

*While affected, each time the entity takes damage it is teleported to a random position within a 7×7×7 area around itself. Andai Canopy can intercept this teleport.*

### 䗖蚀的塌末地城 · Dichou-Collapsed End City

末地城的倒塌版本，生成概率与带末地船的末地城相同，外形就是一座倒塌的末地城。内部部分紫珀块与末地石砖会被替换为䗖蚀紫珀块与䗖蚀末地石砖。

*A collapsed version of an End City. Its generation chance matches that of an End City with an End Ship; as the name suggests, it looks like an End City that has fallen in on itself. Some Purpur Block and End Stone Bricks inside are replaced with Dichou-Infested Purpur Block and Dichou-Infested End Stone Bricks.*

### 䗖蚀方块 · Dichou-Infested Block

被挖掘后有 60% 概率生成末影螨、40% 概率生成䗖蜓；用附有精准采集的物品挖掘会掉落方块本身。

*When mined, it spawns an Endermite 60% of the time and a Dichou Dragonfly 40% of the time. A Silk Touch item mines it and drops the block itself.*

### 䗖蜓 · Dichou Dragonfly

一种紫色虫类生物，身体由四节动画体节组成，每一节都有一对蜻蜓般的翅膀。碰撞箱较小，与蝙蝠相近，位于动画第 2~3 节处。

攻击与行为方式与幻翼十分相似，但攻击时滑行速度约为幻翼的 3 倍。它攻击后附加的效果与它的攻击节奏有关：被它击中的生物会获得「䗖锈」效果（特殊，一级，5 秒）；已带有该效果的生物被击中时不会再次获得。䗖蜓通常在每次攻击后间隔3秒左右后才会再次向目标发起攻击。

击败后 80% 概率掉落「䗖液囊」。䗖蜓与潜影贝敌对。受到岾地花特殊范围影响时，它会优先尝试钻进最近的末地石砖或紫珀块内，放弃其他一切行为。

*A purple insect-like mob with a four-segment animated body, each segment bearing a pair of dragonfly-like wings. Its hitbox is small, similar to a bat’s, around animated segments 2–3.*

*Its attacks and behaviour closely resemble a Phantom’s, but it glides about three times as fast when attacking. The effect it applies on hit is tied to its attack cadence: creatures it hits gain “Dichou Rust” (special, Level I, 5 seconds), and creatures that already have it do not receive it again. After each strike, a Dichou Dragonfly typically waits about 3 seconds before attacking its target again.*

*On death it drops a “Dichou Sac” 80% of the time. Dichou Dragonflies are hostile to Shulkers. When inside a Jiedi Flower’s field, it prioritises burrowing into the nearest End Stone Bricks or Purpur Block, abandoning all other behaviour.*

### 䗖液囊 · Dichou Sac

可在熔炉中烧炼成「䗖结石」。

*Smelts in a furnace into “Dichou Stone”.*

### 䗖锈 · Dichou Rust

拥有该效果者，此后每过 1 秒，受到的伤害翻一倍（×2）；当倍率达到该等级对应的上限（见「䗖锈药水」）后重置，并从 ×1 重新开始循环，直到效果持续时间结束。

*While affected, the damage the entity takes doubles every second. Once the multiplier reaches its cap for that level (see Potion of Dichou Rust), it resets and the cycle begins again from ×1, until the effect’s duration ends.*

### 䗖结石 · Dichou Stone

一种材料，可用于酿造䗖锈药水；五颗䗖结石加四枚紫水晶碎片可合成一块䗖锭。

*A crafting material used to brew Potion of Dichou Rust. Five Dichou Stones plus four Amethyst Shards craft one Dichou Ingot.*

### 䗖锈药水 · Potion of Dichou Rust

以䗖结石为酿造材料酿出的深紫色药水，同样有三种形态——可饮用、喷溅、滞留——共三级：

一级：倍率达到上限所需 3 秒，持续 3 分钟；

二级：倍率达到上限所需 5 秒，持续 5 分钟；

三级：倍率达到上限所需 7 秒，持续 4 分钟。

*A dark purple potion brewed with Dichou Stone. It likewise comes in three forms — drinkable, splash and lingering — and has three levels:*

*Level I: the multiplier caps after 3 seconds; lasts 3 minutes.*

*Level II: the multiplier caps after 5 seconds; lasts 5 minutes.*

*Level III: the multiplier caps after 7 seconds; lasts 4 minutes.*

### 䗖锭 · Dichou Ingot

可在锻造台中配合装饰性盔甲纹饰锻造模板，作为特殊的深紫色末影粒子图案装饰，附加到任意装备上。该装饰不会被之后附加的其他矿物装饰覆盖。

带有䗖锭装饰的护甲，其穿戴者此后每受到一次伤害，护甲防御值就在当前值基础上增加 5%，作为受击后的防御值；下一次受击时，再以增加后的值为基数增加 5%，依此类推。停止受击 3 秒后，所有额外增加的防御值清除，重置为装备本身的基础防御值。

增加值随身上带有䗖锭装饰的装备数量变化，每件增加 5%：例如只穿带装饰的靴子时，每次受击增加 5%；再加一件护腿或胸甲，每次受击增加 10%。

*Can be applied to any equipment at a Smithing Table together with a decorative armour-trim smithing template, adding a special dark purple End-particle pattern. This decoration is not overwritten by other mineral decorations applied later.*

*For armour bearing the Dichou Ingot decoration, each time the wearer takes damage the armour’s defence value increases by 5% of its current value, and that becomes the post-hit defence value; on the next hit it increases by 5% of the already-increased value, and so on. Three seconds after the wearer stops taking damage, all bonus defence is cleared and the armour resets to its base value.*

*The increase scales with the number of decorated pieces worn — 5% per piece: boots alone give 5% per hit; adding leggings or a chestplate raises it to 10% per hit.*
